Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Miya Miya Restaurant, accessible from https://www.miyamiya.in/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Miya Miya Restaurant and how we use it.

Information We Collect

When you fill out a table reservation form or get in touch with us via WhatsApp, we collect personal information you provide, such as your full name, phone number, email address, preferred date/time, and any special dining instructions.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to verify and confirm reservation details, communicate with you directly, optimize restaurant staffing, and improve our services.

Log Files

Miya Miya Restaurant follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.
