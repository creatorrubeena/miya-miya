Best Restaurant in Malappuram

More Than
Just a Meal.

At Miya Miya, we believe that a meal is never just about the food on the plate. It is about the conversation that follows, the laughter that fills the room, and the memories that linger long after the last bite.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen is the result of carefully selected ingredients, time-honoured recipes, and the kind of quiet dedication that only a true passion for food can produce. Widely regarded as the best biryani restaurant in Malappuram, our signature Malabar biryani is slow-cooked with hand-ground spices and the finest Jeerakasala rice — a recipe perfected through years of craft. From that iconic biryani to the richness of our Arabian specialities, we cook with care and we serve with pride.

Miya Miya is not just a restaurant in Malappuram. It is a gathering place — for families, for celebrations, for the simple joy of sitting down together over good food.
